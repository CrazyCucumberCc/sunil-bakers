FAQ

Common customer questions, answered clearly.

A bakery owner is more likely to value the site when it reduces repeated customer questions and makes ordering easier.

Do you make eggless cakes?

Yes. Sunil Bakers offers eggless celebration cakes with the same focus on softness, richness, and presentation.

How early should I enquire for a theme cake?

Advance discussion is best for custom themes so colors, message details, and finishing can be planned properly.

Can names, toppers, and custom writing be added?

Yes. Share the name, occasion, preferred colors, and inspiration so the cake can be tailored for the event.

How is custom pricing decided?

Final pricing depends on size, flavor, eggless preference, finishing detail, and theme complexity.

Is pickup possible for local orders?

Yes. Customers can call or WhatsApp to confirm handover timing and availability for local celebrations.

What details should I send for a fast quotation?

Send the cake type, occasion, flavor, date needed, eggless preference, and theme idea for the fastest response.
