Refund Policy

Refund Policy

Because cakes are freshly prepared and often customized, confirmed orders are generally non-refundable.

Cancellation

Cancellation requests should be communicated as early as possible. Refund eligibility, if any, depends on order status, preparation stage, and customization level.

Quality Concerns

If you have a genuine issue with your order, please contact us immediately with complete details so we can review and support you fairly.

Custom Orders

Theme cakes and personalized cakes often involve advance planning and design work, so they may not qualify for refunds once production begins.
